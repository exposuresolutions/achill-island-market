    THE BAND

    Back to the roots

    A Las Raices — "to the roots" — bring the Gipsy Fiesta to the Costa Blanca: Rumba Flamenca guitars, clarinet, darbuka, conga and cajon, played live by up to nine musicians. From La Zenia and Cabo Roig to Torrevieja and beyond, they turn a quiet afternoon into a party.
